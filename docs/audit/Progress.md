## Current state — 2026-09-30

**Phase:** post-confirmatory audit complete; the registered cold-start protocol completed on 2026-09-27 and its outputs remain locked. The exploratory C101–C106 search is closed without promoting a replacement method.

**Reconstruction.** FedORBIT studies how collaboration affects benign-only anomaly detection on a held-out IoT device with 30–1000 benign support windows. It compares local normalizers with peer marginal moments, covariance sharing, partner selection, and a secondary FedAvg autoencoder. The device is the independent unit. Primary data are nine physical N-BaIoT devices; Gotham2025 is a simulated boundary set with two eligible devices.

**Starting maturity:** implementation/evidence **strong** for the registered within-cohort question; publication contribution **developing**. The completed results show shared marginals help over local baselines on this cohort, the advantage declines with support, richer covariance does not consistently add value, deployable partner selection underperforms all/random partners, and no material channel effect appears on the two eligible simulated targets. See `outputs/analysis.json`, `results/`, and `docs/FedORBIT_Roadmap.md`.

**Attack-capable physical-testbed screen.** CyberFlowIoT-GICAP (2026) is a possible external cohort: its article reports 3,617,388 flows (2,875,481 benign; 741,907 attack) from a physical Burgos lab with Raspberry Pi nodes, attack-window ground truth, and CC BY 4.0 data containing flow files, raw captures, metadata, and manifests.
